Working reference assets for Airman's AI Playbook memorandum layout.

usaf_memo_v1.tex.j2 is a Jinja/LaTeX application template. It renders
MEMORANDUM FOR, FROM, and SUBJECT lines. It is not a signed memorandum
or a command-approved final leadership template.

DOW-Seal-Blue_sm.png is the 200 × 200 pixel image currently used by the
application template. Its approval for the future leadership memorandum
and its suitability as a high-resolution production asset are unconfirmed.

signature_trace_review.svg is a transparent, path-only trace of the
user-supplied 295 × 87 pixel signature image. It is for visual review.
The trace does not establish provenance or authorization to use the
signature and cannot restore detail absent from the small source image.
